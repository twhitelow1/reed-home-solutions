## Voice summary
Reed Home Solutions sounds like a trusted neighbor who happens to be a seasoned contractor: steady, plainspoken
and specific. Clear numbers, honest timelines and short sentences over marketing polish or jargon. Respect the
homeowner's intelligence, explain the "why" behind the work, never oversell.
**Calm competence on a job site — not a sales pitch in a showroom.**

| Attribute | Means | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plainspoken | Everyday language. If a framer wouldn't say it on site, we don't write it. | "We'll have the demo done by Friday." | "We'll execute the deconstruction phase in alignment with projected milestones." |
| Steady | Calm and grounded. No hype, no panic, no overselling. | "Here's what the damage actually looks like, and here's what it'll take to fix it." | "You won't BELIEVE what we found!" |
| Specific | Real numbers, real dates, real next steps. Vagueness is the enemy. | "Rough-in Monday, inspection Wednesday, drywall Friday." | "We'll get to it soon." |
| Accountable | Say what we'll do, then do it. Own timelines, decisions and mistakes. | "That took us two days longer than I quoted. Here's why, and what it means for the finish date." | "Unforeseen circumstances have impacted our deliverables." |

## USP
**One-liner:** Reed Home Solutions is the Colorado mountain-town contractor who finishes what he starts, keeps you
in the loop daily, and works your insurance claim alongside you so your remodel is fully funded — not just
partially covered.

**Elevator pitch:** Most homeowners dealing with damage or a major remodel get stuck in two traps: contractors who
ghost mid-project, and insurance payouts that don't cover the real scope of work. Reed Home Solutions solves both.
We handle full home remodels from demo to final detail across Eagle, Summit, Routt and Pitkin counties, and we
work directly with your adjuster to document the full scope so the payout matches the actual damage. You'll know
what's happening every day, and we don't leave until you're satisfied with the result.

**Differentiators (weave into every page):**
1. Daily communication — homeowners are never left guessing on progress.
2. Hands-on insurance claim support — documentation and scope work directly with the adjuster.
3. Finishes the punch list — the job isn't done until the homeowner says it's done.
4. One GC across four high-country counties (Eagle, Summit, Routt, Pitkin).

### Compliance guardrail (important)
The USP doc says "fights your insurance claim" and "maximize your payout". In Colorado, negotiating a claim on a
homeowner's behalf is the job of a **licensed public adjuster**, and roofing contractors may not pay or waive a
deductible. On the website we keep the promise but phrase it as what a contractor may do: **document the damage,
write a complete itemized scope, walk it through with the adjuster, and submit supplements** so the approved
scope matches the real damage. Never write "we negotiate your claim", "we fight the insurance company", or
"we maximize your payout". Have counsel confirm before using stronger language in ads.

## Audience segments
- Homeowners mid-claim (water, freeze, storm damage)
- Buyers and sellers on tight closing timelines (inspection repairs, pre-listing work)
- Real estate agents
- Property managers
- Trade partners (plumbing, mechanical) who need a reliable GC

## Local SEO targeting
- **Primary (confirmed by Todd 2026-10-08):** Eagle County only (Vail, Avon, Beaver Creek, Edwards, Eagle, Gypsum, Dotsero).
- **Secondary:** Summit County (Breckenridge, Frisco, Dillon, Silverthorne, Keystone, Copper), Garfield County
  (Glenwood Springs, Carbondale), Routt County / Steamboat Springs and Pitkin County / Aspen (plus Snowmass, Basalt). Service-area
  pages, not GBP targets — local pack ranking outside the home county is unrealistic without a physical presence.
- **Core intents:** full home remodels, kitchen/bath remodels, water/storm-damage restoration remodels,
  insurance-claim-funded repairs, deck repair/build, interior finish work.
- **Gap to own:** high-country remodel + insurance claim support. Vail/Summit competitors (Coleman Custom Homes,
  Timberlee & Co., Art Vail Builders, Sundial, Travis Construction) sell luxury custom homes; national restoration
  brands (BluSky, Paul Davis) own "damage restoration" but feel corporate.
- **Keyword types:** city + service modifiers (Vail, Edwards, Breckenridge, Frisco, Steamboat, Aspen) beat broad
  "Colorado" terms. "Near me" auto-localizes, so GBP strength matters more than exact-match "near me" copy.
